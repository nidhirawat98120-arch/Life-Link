About Life-Link

Technology for awareness, connection and organ donation.

What is Life-Link?

Life-Link is a student-developed frontend web project designed to demonstrate how technology can be used to organize information related to organ donation.

The platform provides interfaces for donor registration, organ requests, hospital information and user dashboards.

Our Goal

The goal of Life-Link is to create a simple and user-friendly digital experience that promotes awareness about organ donation.

The project focuses on clean design, easy navigation and interactive frontend features.
